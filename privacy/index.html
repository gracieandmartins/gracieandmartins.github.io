Privacy notice

Last updated 13 November 2026

This is a short interim notice. A fuller privacy notice will replace it once it has been reviewed.

Who we are

This website is operated by Gracie & Martins Partners LLP, based in Lagos, Nigeria. For any question about your personal data, write to advisory@gracieandmartins.com.

This website

This website has no forms and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. It is hosted by GitHub and delivered through Cloudflare. Like any website host, these services process technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver the site and keep it secure, and Cloudflare may set cookies that are strictly necessary for security.

When you write to us

If you email us, your message passes through Cloudflare's email routing service and is stored in our Google email account. We use your name, email address and what you tell us only to reply to you and, if we go on to work together, to manage that work. We do not sell your information or use it for marketing.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or raise a concern at any time by writing to advisory@gracieandmartins.com. You may also contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
