Who we are

This website is operated by Gracie & Martins Partners LLP, a limited liability partnership registered in Nigeria and based in Lagos. In this notice, "we" and "us" mean Gracie & Martins Partners. If you have any question about how we handle your personal data, write to advisory@gracieandmartins.com.

What this website collects

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking tools, and it has no forms. We do not collect personal data through the website itself.

Our website is hosted by GitHub. When anyone visits a site hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub records the visitor's IP address for security purposes. We do not have access to those records and do not use them. GitHub's handling of that information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Information you send us by email

If you write to us, we receive your name, email address and anything you choose to include in your message. We use this only to reply to you, to consider whether we can help, and, if we go on to work together, to prepare a proposal and manage our relationship. We rely on our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and, where relevant, on the steps needed before entering into a contract with you.

Please do not send us sensitive personal data, such as information about health, religion or political views, unless we have asked for it and explained why.

Where your information is stored

Our email is provided by Google Workspace, so messages you send us are stored on Google's systems, which may be located outside Nigeria. We rely on Google's contractual and security commitments to protect that information.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data or share it for marketing. We do not share your enquiry outside the firm without your agreement, except where the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

If we do not go on to work together, we keep your correspondence for 24 months and then delete it. If you become a client, we keep records for as long as our relationship and our legal obligations require.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to be told how your personal data is used, to access it, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw any consent you have given. To exercise any of these rights, write to advisory@gracieandmartins.com. We will respond within the time the law allows.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Changes to this notice

We will update this notice if the way we handle personal data changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
