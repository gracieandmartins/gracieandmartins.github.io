What we do

We work across three disciplines and treat them as one piece of work, because each one weakens when it is handled alone. Public relations builds how you are understood in public, and media engagement earns you a fair hearing with the journalists and editors who shape that understanding. Brand strategy decides what that understanding should be. Each piece of work answers the same questions about what position you need to hold and what it will take to earn it.

Selected work

Evanna Empowerment Initiative

EEI trains bakers and small enterprise owners in Lagos. For its Bakers Product Support initiative, we built the communications around one idea, practical support placed directly with the people already doing the work. A post-event press release took the story to the media, a blog post gave it a longer life, and social content on X, TikTok and LinkedIn kept it in front of EEI's own audience.

We committed to six media mentions and delivered thirteen, spread across tier-one, tier-two and tier-three platforms.

MOD-II

We led the media and content strategy for MOD-II, a Lagos interior design firm moving from residential into commercial and hospitality design. The aim was for the firm and its co-founders to be taken seriously by the people who commission large commercial projects. We planned and developed two features grounded in local market data, an introduction to the firm on Pulse Nigeria and a founder profile on Business Insider Africa, and supported the firm's social media and newsletter content.
