Strategy first. Then the story.

Most communications problems are decisions that were never made. Our approach is designed to make those decisions early and explicitly, so that everything we produce for you afterwards has a clear reason to exist.

  1. Understand

    We start by listening to your leadership, your partners and, where we can, the people you want to reach. We look at how you are currently seen, what you want to be known for, and the distance between the two. We also look closely at what could go wrong, so the strategy accounts for its risks from the start.

  2. Position

    We then agree with you the position your organisation should hold. It must be credible given what you actually do, distinct from the alternatives your audience is weighing, and valuable to the people whose decisions affect your future. We put this in writing so it can guide every choice that follows.

  3. Shape the narrative

    With the position agreed, we build the narrative that supports it. That includes the story you tell, the evidence that makes it believable, the voices who should carry it, and the moments when it should be told.

  4. Earn and hold attention

    We put the plan to work through media engagement, events, partnerships and owned channels, and we review results against what we set out to achieve. Reputations are earned slowly and lost quickly, so we keep counsel close to the work long after launch.

What you can expect from us

We test our own work before you see it, looking for gaps in the logic and assumptions that have not been checked. When something is uncertain, we say so and ask. Every document we deliver is written to be understood on its own, by anyone in your organisation who reads it. And we keep what you share with us in confidence, during our work together and after it ends.
