Three disciplines, one question.

Every engagement begins with the same question, which is what position your organisation needs to hold and what it will take to earn it. The services below are the ways we help you answer it. Most clients need more than one, and we will tell you honestly when you need less than you expected.

Brand strategy

We help you decide what your organisation should be known for and how to express it consistently. That includes defining your position against the alternatives your audience is weighing, the story that supports it, and the principles that keep your messages, visual identity and conduct aligned. We leave you with a strategy your whole team can put to use in its daily work.

Public relations

We plan and manage how your organisation is presented in public, from launches and announcements to sensitive moments that need careful handling. Our work covers narrative development, press materials, spokesperson preparation and the judgement calls about what to say, when, and through whom.

Media engagement

We build considered relationships with journalists, editors and broadcasters, so your organisation is understood accurately when it matters. That means offering stories with real news value, preparing your spokespeople to be clear under questioning, and protecting your reputation when coverage does not go your way.

How engagements work

We work on defined projects and on ongoing retainers. Every engagement starts with a conversation about what you need to achieve, followed by a written proposal that sets out our understanding, our recommended approach, the scope of work and the terms. We do not begin work until both sides have agreed those terms in writing.
