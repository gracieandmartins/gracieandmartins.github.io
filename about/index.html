A young firm, built deliberately.

Gracie & Martins Partners is a limited liability partnership founded in Lagos by two partners who believe that reputation is earned through clear, deliberate decisions. We started the firm because too many organisations communicate before they have decided what they want to be known for, and spend heavily on visibility that does not move how they are seen.

What we believe

A position is a decision your whole organisation commits to and acts on. Before we write a word for a client, we want to understand what they need to be known for, by whom, and why that matters to their future. The narrative follows from that decision, and every campaign, media conversation and partnership is judged by whether it strengthens the position or dilutes it.

We also believe good counsel shows its reasoning. Our recommendations come with the thinking behind them, the risks we see, and the questions that remain open, so our clients can weigh our advice and make informed decisions.

How we are built

We have set up the firm to the governance standards expected of established international consultancies, from the way we contract with clients to the way we protect their information. We treat confidentiality as part of how we work every day. We do not discuss client work publicly without consent.

We work with a trusted network of specialists, including writers, designers, photographers and videographers. Each one works under our standards and our confidentiality terms, and the partners remain accountable for everything we deliver.

Where we work

We are based in Lagos and work with organisations across Nigeria. We are particularly well placed to support charities and foundations, growing Nigerian businesses, companies running corporate social responsibility and sponsorship programmes, and organisations new to the Nigerian market that need to be understood quickly and accurately.
